What to do before your workshop

Four steps. They mean the three hours in the room go into building, not deciding what to build.

STEP 1

Find your workshop

Select it in the list above. Names from the sign-up form are already there.

STEP 2

Confirm your name

Select "This is me". Picked the wrong name? Select "This isn't me". Not on the list? Add your name.

STEP 3

Pick a partner

You work in pairs. Agree with your partner first, then one of you selects the other. Ideally, pair with someone who shares a client with you.

STEP 4

Submit your problem list

Describe the problem and how you think you will solve it. Only facilitators can read it.

What makes a good one

Name a real task from last week, not a category. "Pulling coverage links into the monthly report" is better than "reporting".

Say how long it takes now. That is how you will measure what you built.

Keep confidential client details out. Describe the shape of the data, not the data.

Changed your mind?

Submit again. Your latest version replaces the earlier one. A copy of your answers stays on this device so you can edit them.
